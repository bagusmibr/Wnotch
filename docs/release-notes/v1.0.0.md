## Wnotch 1.0.0

**🇬🇧 English** · [🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia](#-bahasa-indonesia)

First release of Wnotch: synced Spotify lyrics, right in your notch.

### What's new
- 🎤 **Spotify lyrics in the notch**: while Spotify plays, the notch shows only the current lyric line, synced to the song and back in sync about a second after you skip forward or back.
- 🔎 **More songs found**: titles like "- Remastered 2011", "(feat. …)" or "- From … Soundtrack" are cleaned before searching, and busy lyrics servers are retried.
- 🕘 **Never empty**: when nothing is playing, the notch shows the time and battery.
- 🏝️ **No notch? No problem**: on Macs without a notch and on external monitors, Wnotch floats as an island at the top of the screen.
- 🎵 Now Playing, live activities (downloads, AirDrop, timers, screen recording, Focus, Hotspot), battery/Bluetooth/Wi-Fi/VPN alerts and custom volume/brightness HUD.

### Install
1. Download **Wnotch.dmg** below and drag **Wnotch** into **Applications**.
2. Wnotch is not notarized yet, so run once in Terminal:
   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Wnotch.app
   ```
3. Open Wnotch and allow **Automation → Spotify** and **Accessibility**.

Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later** (Apple silicon and Intel). **Windows: coming soon.**

### 🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia

Rilis pertama Wnotch: lirik Spotify yang tersinkron, langsung di notch kamu.

#### Yang baru
- 🎤 **Lirik Spotify di notch**: saat Spotify diputar, notch hanya menampilkan baris lirik yang sedang dinyanyikan, dan kembali pas sekitar satu detik setelah lagu dimajukan atau dimundurkan.
- 🔎 **Lebih banyak lagu ditemukan**: judul seperti "- Remastered 2011", "(feat. …)" atau "- From … Soundtrack" dibersihkan sebelum dicari, dan server lirik yang sibuk dicoba ulang otomatis.
- 🕘 **Tidak pernah kosong**: saat tidak ada lagu, notch menampilkan jam dan baterai.
- 🏝️ **Tanpa notch pun bisa**: di Mac tanpa notch dan monitor eksternal, Wnotch tampil sebagai kapsul mengambang di atas layar.
- 🎵 Now Playing, aktivitas langsung (unduhan, AirDrop, timer, rekam layar, Focus, Hotspot), notifikasi baterai/Bluetooth/Wi-Fi/VPN, serta HUD volume/kecerahan.

#### Cara pasang
1. Unduh **Wnotch.dmg** di bawah, lalu seret **Wnotch** ke **Applications**.
2. Wnotch belum dinotarisasi, jadi jalankan sekali di Terminal:
   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Wnotch.app
   ```
3. Buka Wnotch dan izinkan **Automation → Spotify** serta **Accessibility**.

Butuh **macOS 14 Sonoma atau lebih baru** (Apple silicon dan Intel). **Windows: segera hadir.**
